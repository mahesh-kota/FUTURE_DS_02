**# Customer Retention \& Churn Analysis Dashboard**



**## Objective**

**Analyze customer churn patterns and retention trends for a subscription-based business using Python and Tableau.**



**---**



**## Tools Used**

**- Python**

**- Pandas**

**- Jupyter Notebook**

**- Tableau Public**



**---**



**## Project Workflow**

**1. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing**

**2. Churn Rate Calculation**

**3. Customer Retention Analysis**

**4. Contract Type Analysis**

**5. Internet Service Analysis**

**6. Monthly Charges Analysis**

**7. Customer Tenure Analysis**

**8. Dashboard Creation in Tableau**



**---**



**## Dashboard Features**

**- Total Customers KPI**

**- Churn Rate KPI**

**- Retention Rate KPI**

**- Churn by Contract Type**

**- Churn by Internet Service**

**- Monthly Charges Analysis**

**- Customer Tenure Analysis**



**---**



**## Key Insights**

**- Month-to-month customers showed the highest churn rate.**

**- Customers with shorter tenure were more likely to leave.**

**- Fiber optic users had higher churn rates compared to DSL users.**

**- Retention rate remained above 70%.**



**---**



**## Business Recommendations**

**- Encourage customers to move to long-term contracts.**

**- Improve onboarding experience for new customers.**

**- Provide loyalty offers for high-risk customers.**

**- Monitor customers with higher monthly charges.**
